# Hi, I'm Aftab Rehan

### Frontend Developer & UI/UX Designer

I bring design and development together to build thoughtful web experiences. I enjoy shaping how a product looks, how it feels, and how it works—from the first interface concept to the finished application.

My work spans collaborative whiteboards, SaaS applications, AI tools, and product interfaces. React, Next.js, and TypeScript are at the core of what I build, with a focus on turning complex features into clear, usable experiences.

Based in **Multan, Pakistan** · Trying my best to merge **tech & art**.
